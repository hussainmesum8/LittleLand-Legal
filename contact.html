Common questions

How do I get LittleLand Pro back on a new device?
Open the parent area and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.
How do I cancel my subscription?
On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
How do I open the parent area?
Tap the lock at the top of the home screen and answer the question with the number pad.
