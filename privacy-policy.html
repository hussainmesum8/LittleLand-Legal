Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

LittleLand is a coloring app for children aged 2 to 6. It has no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Your child's pictures, names and progress stay on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else.

1. Who we are

LittleLand is made by Mohammad Mesum Hussain Qureshi, Pakistan. Contact: hussainmesum8@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

Child profiles (name, age and avatar), pictures, progress and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. On iPhone and iPad they may be part of your own iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes everything.

3. What leaves your device

Purchases. Apple or Google handle payment; we never see your payment details. RevenueCat confirms your purchase using your store purchase records and a random app identifier, not linked to you and never used for tracking.

Crash reports. Only if you allowed it in your device settings, Apple or Google may send us anonymous crash reports.

Nothing else is sent. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

4. Permissions

The app only asks to add pictures to your photo library, when a grown-up saves a finished picture. It cannot read your photos. It does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

5. Children

LittleLand is made for children aged 2 to 6. We do not collect personal information from children. Purchases, settings, printing and sharing are behind a parental gate.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete your data, including purchase records held by RevenueCat, by emailing hussainmesum8@gmail.com.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
